# What the series show (as of the 2026-08-21 pull)

Read with the seams in mind: GSS 2021+ is web-first, NSDUH 2021+ is a new baseline, Michigan moved online 2017–2024, Gallup's well-being index moved to a web panel 2018–2020.

## Happiness and outlook

- **Very happy** (GSS): 23.4% in 2024 — 33 of 35 readings since 1972 were higher; the pre-2021 floor was 28% (2010). The drop coincides with the 2021 mode change, and has not recovered in two later waves.
- **Optimistic about life in 5 years** (Gallup, %8–10): 59.2% in 2025, the lowest in the series; 2026 year-to-date 59.7%.
- **Satisfied with how things are going in the U.S.** (Gallup annual mean): 25% so far in 2026; the series has not averaged above 40% since 2004.
- **Better off than parents**: 54.5% (2024), down from 65% in 1994. **Kids will be better off**: 58.8% (2024), roughly where it was in 1994 after a 2000 peak near 69%.
- **Expect to be better off next year** (Michigan): 26% in 2025, down from 32% in 2024; the series low is 22% (1979), the high 43% (2000).

## Trust

- **Most people can be trusted** (GSS): 24.8% (2024), the lowest of 30 readings since 1972 (43% in 1976).
- **Trust in mass media** (Gallup): 28% (2025), lowest on record; 68–72% in the 1970s.
- Gallup confidence, 2026: military 61, small business 67, police 45, organized religion 33, medical system 28, banks 28, Supreme Court 27, presidency 27, public schools 27, big business 17, newspapers 17, TV news 14, Congress 9.
- GSS confidence in **science** ("great deal"): 36.3% in 2024, its lowest reading since 1973; in **medicine**: 26.3%, down from 55% in 1973.

## Substance use

- **Adult marijuana use, past year** (NSDUH, 12+): 21.2% (2025); 11.0% in 2002.
- **Teen marijuana use** (12th grade, past 30 days): 16.2% (2024), versus 33.7% in 1980; teen alcohol 21.7% (72.0% in 1980), teen cigarettes 2.5% (30.5%).
- **Per-capita alcohol**: 2.48 gallons of ethanol (2023), up about 10% from the 2010 low (2.26) and below the 1981 peak (2.76).

## Health access and mortality

- **Couldn't afford prescriptions**: 5.6% in 2019 when the item was retired (peak 8.4% in 2009); the replacement adult item runs 7.8% in 2024.
- **Suicide, ages 15–24**: 13.2 per 100,000 (2024), about 40% above the 2007 low (9.6) and below the 2021 peak (15.2); **ages 45–64**: 18.7; **ages 10–14**: 2.3, about 2.5× the 2007 level (0.9).
- **Infant mortality**: 5.5 per 1,000 (2024), near the record low of 5.4 (2020); 99.9 in 1915.
- **Uninsured**: 8.2% (2024) vs 14.6% in 2008.
- **Traffic deaths per 100M miles**: 1.19 (2024); 3.25 in 1976.

## Household financial stress

- **Credit card serious delinquency** (NY Fed, 90+ days): 13.0% of balances in 2026 so far, just under the 2010 peak (13.5%) and up from 7.3% in 2016. The Fed Board's 30+ day bank-card rate is 2.9%, lower than its 1991 start (5.4%).
- **Consumer bankruptcies**: 501.6 thousand in 2025, about a quarter of the 2010 level (2.1 million).
- **Prime-age employment**: 80.6% (2026 YTD), near the all-time high. **Union membership**: 10% (2025), from 20% in 1983.
- **Child poverty**: 14.3% (2024). **On SNAP**: 12.4% of residents (FY2025). **Food insecurity**: 13.7% of households (2024), the highest since 2014.
