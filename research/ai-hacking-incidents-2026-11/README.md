# AI-Assisted Cyberattacks in Public Reporting (2023-01 to 2026-11)

**Question:** which cyberattacks have been publicly reported as using AI by the attacker, and how strong is the evidence for each report?

**Answer (2026-11-17):** 286 incidents are on file: 145 Confirmed, 109 Strong and 32 Suspected, from 244 swept sources (278 in the registry: 244 swept, 25 unreachable, 9 cited only) and 371 reviewed candidates. This is a count of published reports, not of attacks, and it is a floor. The sweep found 58 of 70 incidents on an independent held-out list (83%); the other 12 were found separately, so the sweep alone would have missed about 17% of what that list named. Attribution belongs to the sources. What large vendors and model providers can see on their own platforms is over-represented.

Published as [hackingincidents.ai](https://hackingincidents.ai). Dataset: [Data/AI-Hacking-Incidents](../../Data/AI-Hacking-Incidents/README.md).
