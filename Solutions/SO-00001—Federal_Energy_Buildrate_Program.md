## Approach

Target the **rate**, not the total. Capacity targets invite arithmetic that ignores supply chains.
A rate ladder makes the constraint visible every year, and every rung is falsifiable on schedule.

**End state:** ~3,160 GW installed, **2.4×** the derived 2025 baseline of ~1,300 GW.

**Cost:** 1,860 GW × $1.5B/GW blended capex = $2.79T, plus 50% for transmission, storage and
interconnection = **$4.19T over ten years, $419B/yr.** Blended capex derived from
[EIA construction costs](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=60562) (solar $1,588/kW,
wind $1,451/kW, gas $820/kW, 2022).

## The four unlock programs

Funded and started **before** the generation capex scales, because each one gates the ladder.

### U1 — Cell Independence

**Target:** 60 GW/yr of domestic solar cell capacity by 2030.

The US has 69.9 GW/yr of module capacity and 3 GW/yr of cell capacity
([SEIA](https://seia.org/news/united-states-surpasses-50-gw-of-solar-module-manufacturing-capacity/)).
Announced-but-unbuilt: 56 GW cells, 24 GW wafers, 13 GW ingots. Underwrite the announced plans with
offtake guarantees rather than new credits — the credits already produced a 750% increase in module
capacity and did not produce cells.

### U2 — Transformer Surge

**Target:** large-transformer lead times under 52 weeks by 2030, under 26 weeks by 2036.

Current: 128 weeks, 144 for generator step-up units, up to four years for high-capacity, prices
+77% since 2019, 30% modeled national shortfall
([POWER](https://www.powermag.com/transformers-in-2026-shortage-scramble-or-self-inflicted-crisis/)).
Requires a grain-oriented electrical steel program, not just transformer plants; GOES is the
upstream constraint.

### U3 — Queue Reform

**Target:** median interconnection request to commercial operation under 24 months by 2030.

2,061 GW is already queued — more than the entire installed fleet — with median time over 4 years
and 750 GW withdrawn in 2025
([LBNL](https://emp.lbl.gov/publications/queued-2025-edition-characteristics)). This is the cheapest
GW in the plan: it is already funded, already sited, already designed. Process reform converts it
at near-zero capital cost.

### U4 — One Million Electricians

**Target:** 100,000 new electricians and line workers per year, every year.

DOL projects 81,000 electrician openings a year for a decade as replacement demand alone, plus
10,700 line installers; 59% of utility employers already cannot hire line workers
([CEWD](https://cewd.org/resources/energy-workforce-fast-facts/)). Delivered through
[SO-00003](./SO-00003—Universal_Free_AI_And_Business_Training.md) Tier 3.

## Employment

At the Goldman Sachs ratio of 507,000 workers per 300 GW (**1,690 workers/GW**), 1,860 GW implies
**~3.14 million direct energy workers**
([via EnergyNow](https://energynow.com/2026/05/data-center-rush-worsens-shortages-of-power-grid-workers/)).

## What would falsify this solution

- Any ladder rung missed by more than 20% two years running.
- Domestic cell capacity below 20 GW/yr in 2030.
- Median interconnection time still above 36 months in 2030.
- Transformer lead times still above 100 weeks in 2030.
- Cost per GW exceeding $2.2B blended, which would break the funding match in
  [CL-00002](../Claims/CL-00002—Named_Funding_Covers_The_Maximum_Reachable_Buildout.md).
