## Approach

Fund the **component layer**, not the prime contractors. The US can already design drones and
robots. It cannot make the cells, motors, radios, cameras, and precision reducers they are built
from, and a US-assembled machine full of Chinese parts is an import with extra steps.

**Budget: $40B/yr.**

## Drone track

| Target | Current | 2030 | 2036 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imported share of US drone market | ~77% ([ElectroIQ](https://electroiq.com/stats/dji-statistics/)) | 40% | <10% |
| Domestic LFP cell capacity for UAS | insufficient; ≥5 yrs to build ([CEPA](https://cepa.org/article/reality-check-breaking-free-from-chinas-drone-ecosystem-is-harder-than-you-think/)) | military demand met | full civil + military demand |
| Defense units | 150,000 by 2028 ([DroneLife](https://dronelife.com/2026/05/13/drone-dominance-us-drone-supply-chain-xponential-2026/)) | on schedule | sustained production line |

The December 2025 ban on new foreign-manufactured UAS removed the supply. It did not create a
replacement. China produces ~90% of the world's commercial drones and over 70% of global drone
exports ([CEPA](https://cepa.org/article/reality-check-breaking-free-from-chinas-drone-ecosystem-is-harder-than-you-think/)).
Component priorities, in order of how badly the US is exposed: **LFP cells, brushless motors,
radios, optics, airframe composites.**

## Robotics track

**Target: match South Korea's current robot density by 2036.**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| US density now | 307 per 10,000 employees, 8th globally ([IFR](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/robot-density-surges-in-europe-asia-and-americas)) |
| South Korea | 1,220 per 10,000 ([IFR](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/robot-density-surges-in-europe-asia-and-americas)) |
| US manufacturing employment | 12.6M ([BLS Apr 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)) |
| Implied US installed base (derived) | ~387,000 robots |
| Implied base at Korean density (derived) | ~1.54M robots |
| Gap | ~1.15M robots over 10 years |
| Required installation rate | **~115,000/yr** |
| Current installation rate | 38,000/yr, +11% ([IFR](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/us-robot-industry-returns-to-double-digit-growth)) |

**3× the current rate, sustained for a decade.** Automotive already absorbs 13,500 units a year; the
growth has to come from food, metals, machinery, electronics, and logistics — the sectors IFR
reports growing fastest.

## Why this pillar is downstream of energy

Robots and drones are electricity in the shape of labor. Tripling robot density without
[SO-00001](./SO-00001—Federal_Energy_Buildrate_Program.md) means bidding for the same constrained
electricity that data centers are already bidding for.

## Falsifiers

- US robot installations below 60,000/yr in 2030.
- Robot density below 500 per 10,000 in 2032.
- Imported drone share still above 60% in 2030.
- Domestic LFP cell capacity insufficient for the 150,000-unit defense program by 2029.
