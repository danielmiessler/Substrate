## Mechanism

Recover tax on US multinational profit booked in low-tax jurisdictions, through worldwide combined
reporting, enforcement, and minimum-tax alignment.

## Yield

**$100B/yr** (working figure).

| Estimate | Value | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| US MNC profit shifted to 13 tax havens | **$369B** | 2022 | [Zucman & Wier](https://gabriel-zucman.eu/files/WZ2022WIDER.pdf) |
| JCT revenue loss estimate | $83B/yr | 2014 | [literature review](https://gabriel-zucman.eu/files/WZ2022WIDER.pdf) |
| Clausing revenue loss estimate | $90B | 2008 | [literature review](https://gabriel-zucman.eu/files/WZ2022WIDER.pdf) |
| Share of US firms' foreign income booked in havens | 60% (global average 40%) | | [Zucman](https://gabriel-zucman.eu/files/WZ2022WIDER.pdf) |
| Share of global MNC profits shifted to havens | rose from <2% (1970s) to 37% (2019) | | [CEPR](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/new-global-estimates-profits-tax-havens-suggest-tax-loss-continues-rise) |
| Global government revenue loss to havens | ~$200B/yr | | [WEF](https://www.weforum.org/stories/2020/02/how-do-corporate-tax-havens-work/) |

The $100B working figure sits between the JCT ($83B, 2014 basis) and Clausing ($90B, 2008 basis)
estimates, adjusted upward for the growth in shifted profit since — $369B booked in havens in 2022
against a base that was materially smaller in 2008.

## Why this is the least reliable line in the stack

**The estimates are old.** JCT's is on a 2014 basis, Clausing's on 2008. Both predate the 2017 tax
act, GILTI, FDII, and the OECD Pillar Two minimum tax, all of which changed the incentives.

**Recovery is not the same as the estimate.** These figures measure revenue *lost*, not revenue
*recoverable*. Full recovery requires either worldwide combined reporting or effective multilateral
enforcement, and neither has been achieved anywhere at this scale.

**It is the line to cut first.** If the funding stack has to shrink, this is the honest place to
take the loss, because it is the estimate with the widest error bars and the weakest enforcement
path. The stack survives at $473B → $390B, which no longer covers the full
[SO-00001](../Solutions/SO-00001—Federal_Energy_Buildrate_Program.md) ladder and would force the
2034–2036 rungs down.

## Falsifiers

- Post-Pillar Two data showing US MNC haven profit below $150B/yr.
- Enforcement yield under $40B/yr in the first three years of the program.
- Worldwide combined reporting failing legal challenge.
