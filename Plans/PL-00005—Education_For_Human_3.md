# CHALLENGES

1. **Nearly half of graduating seniors cannot do basic math.** Not calculus — basic. This is the
   binding constraint on every other plan in this program, including the 100,000 electricians a year
   that [PL-00002](./PL-00002—US_Buildout_Plan.md) needs.
2. **Money is not the variable.** Per-pupil spending hit a record in the same year outcomes hit a
   floor, and the spread between states does not track outcomes cleanly.
3. **The system optimizes for seat time.** Students advance on a calendar rather than on mastery,
   so a gap opened in third grade is still there in twelfth.
4. **We are teaching for a world that ended.** Even a perfectly executed 1995 curriculum prepares a
   student for jobs that [PL-00006](./PL-00006—Work_And_Income_Through_The_AI_Transition.md)
   documents disappearing.

---

# MISSION

1. Get **below-basic to under 10%** in reading and math at every grade level.
2. Advance students on **demonstrated mastery**, not on age.
3. Graduate people who can think independently, use AI as an instrument, and build something of
   their own — the Human 3.0 target.

---

# STRATEGIES

### S1 — Mastery gates, not grade levels

No student advances past a foundational gate without demonstrating it. **Target: below-basic under
10% by 2036.** This is the unpopular one — it means some students take longer — and it is the only
intervention that addresses the actual mechanism, which is compounding unremediated gaps.

Pair it with unlimited attempts and no stigma. The gate is on the skill, not on the student.

### S2 — One tutor per student

The two-sigma result — individual tutoring moves a median student to the 98th percentile — has been
known since 1984 and was never affordable. **Target: every student has an AI tutor with a human
teacher supervising by 2030.** This is the single highest-leverage application of AI in existence
and it is being spent on advertising optimization instead.

The teacher's job shifts from delivering content to diagnosing, motivating, and handling the things
a machine should not: judgment, conflict, character.

### S3 — Teach how the world actually works

Physical law, evolution, economics, statistics, incentives, propaganda, and how to evaluate a claim.
**Target: a required "how the world works" sequence in every state by 2032.** This is
[PL-00001](./us-plan1-miessler.md)'s education strategy, made concrete: the goal is independent
thinking, not a list of facts.

### S4 — Make building things a graduation requirement

Every student ships something real before they leave: a business, a piece of software, a repair, a
performance, a piece of writing that someone outside the school read. **Target: 100% of graduates
with a portfolio, not just a transcript.**

### S5 — Publish everything

Per-school outcomes, per-dollar, risk-adjusted, on a standard schema. **Target: full outcome
transparency by 2030.** Same reasoning as [PL-00004](./PL-00004—Healthcare_That_Costs_What_Its_Worth.md)
S5: you cannot improve what you are not permitted to compare.

# WHAT CHANGES

1. A high school diploma means the holder can read, compute, and reason. Right now it does not.
2. Every child gets the tutoring that was previously only available to the wealthy.
3. Graduates leave with something they made, not just a record of attendance.
4. The country stops producing the labor shortage that constrains every other plan it writes.

Per-pupil spending is held **flat in real terms** deliberately. If outcomes only move by adding
money, the plan is wrong and should be replaced rather than funded.

---

# HOW THIS FAILS

- **Mastery gates read as retention**, which is politically toxic and historically produced bad
  outcomes when implemented as punishment rather than as support.
- **AI tutors could deliver engagement without learning** — the measurement in S5 is what separates
  a tutor from a very patient distraction.
- **Teacher displacement fear** kills S2 before it starts unless the role shift in S2 is real and
  funded.
- **Federal leverage over local schools is weak and contested**, so most of this happens state by
  state or not at all.

---

# WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THIS PLAN

- AI tutoring deployed at scale showing no effect on below-basic rates within three years.
- Mastery-gate states showing worse long-run outcomes than social-promotion states.
- Below-basic rates responding strongly to per-pupil spending in a controlled comparison, which
  would mean the money thesis is right and this plan's premise is wrong.
