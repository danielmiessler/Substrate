# Plans

Comprehensive, transparent plans for running an entity — a team, a department, a city, a country.
Each plan follows the Telos structure: scope, challenges, mission, strategies, ideal world, metrics.
Plans reference other Substrate objects by ID, so any single piece can be argued with on its own.

---

## The US Program

Seven plans that fit together. They share one premise: America's problems are mostly execution
problems, they are measurable, and a plan that cannot be checked is not a plan.

| ID | Plan | The one-liner |
|----|------|---------------|
| [PL-00001](./us-plan1-miessler.md) | **Shared Identity** | Unify the country around a positive shared identity based on the Bill of Rights. |
| [PL-00002](./PL-00002—US_Buildout_Plan.md) | **The Buildout** | 10× the energy, rebuild the machines that build things, train 50 million adults — paid for by AI capital and recovered corporate tax base. |
| [PL-00003](./PL-00003—Housing_Abundance.md) | **Housing Abundance** | Build 15 million homes in ten years by making it legal to build them. |
| [PL-00004](./PL-00004—Healthcare_That_Costs_What_Its_Worth.md) | **Healthcare** | Pay 2.5× the developed world for 2.7 fewer years of life. Close that gap and it frees ~$1.5T a year. |
| [PL-00005](./PL-00005—Education_For_Human_3.md) | **Education for Human 3.0** | 45% of twelfth graders can't do basic math. Fix the floor, then build the thing worth building. |
| [PL-00006](./PL-00006—Work_And_Income_Through_The_AI_Transition.md) | **Work & Income** | The jobs are going. The work isn't. Move 170 million people from holding jobs to owning capability. |
| [PL-00007](./PL-00007—A_Government_That_Runs_On_Understanding.md) | **Government** | $186B in improper payments, four-year permits, 79% of IT spend keeping old systems alive. Every other plan runs through this one. |
| [PL-00008](./PL-00008—Fiscal_Solvency.md) | **Fiscal Solvency** | Interest hits $1T this year and $2.1T by 2036. Everything else is unaffordable if this fails. |

### How they connect

- **PL-00007 is the enabling plan.** Nothing else executes if permits take four years and $186B a
  year leaks. Buildout, housing, and healthcare all inherit its timelines.
- **PL-00004 is the fiscal plan.** Health spending at 17.2% of GDP is the largest recoverable pool
  in American public life; PL-00008 depends on it more than on any tax change.
- **PL-00005 and PL-00006 are the same pipeline** at two ages. PL-00002 needs 100,000 electricians a
  year and PL-00003 needs 250,000 construction trades; neither exists without them.
- **PL-00002 is the physical layer.** Cheap abundant energy is the denominator lever in PL-00008 and
  the input to everything in PL-00003 and PL-00006.
- **PL-00001 is the one that makes the rest possible.** A country that cannot agree on anything does
  not execute a ten-year program.

## Reading a plan

Every plan in this set holds to the same four rules:

1. **Every number carries a primary source.** EIA, BLS, CBO, JCT, GAO, OECD, Census, NAGB, LBNL,
   IFR, SEIA, NREL, Freddie Mac, or a named paper. Not a summary of a source.
2. **Derived numbers are labeled derived.** Arithmetic on top of a published figure is marked as
   such, every time it is used.
3. **Every plan names what would falsify it.** The last section of each file is the specific
   observations that would prove it wrong.
4. **Each plan argues against itself in public.** The strongest objection lives in the document, in
   a section called *How this fails*, not in the replies.
