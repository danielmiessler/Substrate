# PR-00004 — Corporate Tax Base Erosion

**Category:** Problem
**Scale:** National (United States)
**Created:** 2026-07-24
**Referenced by:** [PL-00002](../Plans/PL-00002—US_Buildout_Plan.md)

## Statement

The federal government forgoes an amount of corporate tax comparable to what it collects, through
statutory expenditures and unaddressed profit shifting.

## Evidence

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|

## The honest counterweight

JCT has found that eliminating **every** corporate tax expenditure would only support lowering the
corporate rate to **28%**
([JCT via MLPA](https://www.mlpassociation.org/joint-committee-on-taxation-report-eliminating-all-corporate-tax-expenditures-only-lowers-rate-to-28/)).
The base recoverable here is real but smaller than the rhetoric around "loopholes" implies, and
several of the largest items — the R&D credit, accelerated depreciation, the energy credit — are
the exact provisions that subsidize the buildout
[PL-00002](../Plans/PL-00002—US_Buildout_Plan.md) proposes. Repealing them to fund it is partly
self-cancelling and the plan must say so.

## Falsifiers

- JCT scoring shows total corporate tax expenditures below $150B in a future year.
- Post-Pillar Two enforcement data shows US MNC haven profit falling below $150B/yr.
- Static and dynamic scoring of full expenditure repeal differ by more than 40%.
