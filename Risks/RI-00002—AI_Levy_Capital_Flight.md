# RI-00002 — The AI Levy Drives the Capital Offshore

**Category:** Risk
**Created:** 2026-07-24
**Applies to:** [FS-00001](../Funding-Sources/FS-00001—AI_Infrastructure_Levy.md)
**Severity:** High
**Likelihood:** Medium

---

## The risk

A 15% levy on AI infrastructure capex taxes the single most mobile large capital flow in the
economy. Datacenters can be built in Ireland, Singapore, or the Gulf. If the levy moves the
buildout offshore, the US loses the tax base, the construction jobs, and the compute.

## Why it is credible

- The base is enormous and concentrated: **$725B** across four firms in 2026, up **77%** from $410B
  ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/chart/35046/capital-expenditure-of-meta-alphabet-amazon-and-microsoft/)).
  Four boardrooms decide the entire yield.
- The same firms already demonstrate aggressive tax-structure mobility: US multinationals book
  **60% of foreign earned income in tax havens** against a global average of 40%
  ([Zucman](https://gabriel-zucman.eu/files/WZ2022WIDER.pdf)).
- The levy lands on capex, which is a location decision, not a profit decision. That is precisely
  what makes the base observable ([FS-00001](../Funding-Sources/FS-00001—AI_Infrastructure_Levy.md))
  and precisely what makes it movable.

## Why likelihood is Medium rather than High

Datacenter siting is constrained by power, water, latency, and fiber, not only by tax. The US
currently offers the deepest power market in the developed world, and the plan's own
[SO-00001](../Solutions/SO-00001—Federal_Energy_Buildrate_Program.md) makes it deeper. A firm
leaving to avoid a 15% capex levy would be trading it for the interconnection queues of a smaller
grid.

## Mitigation

A **US-siting credit** against the levy: the levy applies at 15%, and capex deployed inside the
United States on grid-connected infrastructure earns a credit that reduces the effective rate. This
converts the instrument from a tax on AI into a tax on building AI infrastructure somewhere else.
This mitigation is **not modeled in the plan's $109B yield**, and modeling it would reduce that
figure.

## Leading indicator

Share of Big Four announced datacenter capex sited in the United States, tracked quarterly against
the pre-levy baseline. A decline of more than 10 percentage points in two years is the trigger to
restructure.

## Knock-on

Losing [FS-00001](../Funding-Sources/FS-00001—AI_Infrastructure_Levy.md) drops the funding stack
from $473B/yr to $364B/yr, below the $466B program cost. See
[CL-00002](../Claims/CL-00002—Named_Funding_Covers_The_Maximum_Reachable_Buildout.md).
