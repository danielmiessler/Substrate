# RI-00001 — Funding a Buildrate the Supply Chain Cannot Absorb

**Category:** Risk
**Created:** 2026-07-24
**Applies to:** [PL-00002](../Plans/PL-00002—US_Buildout_Plan.md), [SO-00001](../Solutions/SO-00001—Federal_Energy_Buildrate_Program.md)
**Severity:** High
**Likelihood:** High

---

## The risk

Money entering a supply-constrained market raises prices instead of output. If $419B/yr is
appropriated against a supply chain that can deliver 100 GW/yr, the result is more expensive
turbines, cells, and transformers, not more of them.

## Why the likelihood is High

The constraints are already visibly price-inelastic:

- Transformer prices are **up 77% since 2019** with lead times at **128 weeks** and a modeled 30%
  national shortfall ([POWER](https://www.powermag.com/transformers-in-2026-shortage-scramble-or-self-inflicted-crisis/)).
  Demand for generator step-up transformers rose **274% between 2019 and 2025** and lead times went
  up, not down.
- Federal manufacturing credits produced a **750% increase in solar module capacity** and left cell
  capacity at **3 GW/yr** ([SEIA](https://seia.org/news/united-states-surpasses-50-gw-of-solar-module-manufacturing-capacity/)).
  Capital went to the easy layer of the stack. It will do so again.
- **2,061 GW** sits in interconnection queues already financed, already sited
  ([LBNL](https://emp.lbl.gov/publications/queued-2025-edition-characteristics)). Adding money to
  that queue adds nothing.

## Mitigation built into the plan

[SO-00001](../Solutions/SO-00001—Federal_Energy_Buildrate_Program.md) funds the four unlock programs
— cells, transformers, queue reform, electricians — **before** the generation capex scales, and the
rate ladder rises gradually rather than front-loading. Queue reform in particular is near-zero
capital and converts already-funded projects.

## Leading indicator

**If per-GW installed cost rises faster than the ladder, the program is being absorbed as inflation
rather than capacity.** Track $/kW against the [EIA construction cost](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=60562)
series annually. A blended figure above $2.2B/GW breaks the funding match in
[CL-00002](../Claims/CL-00002—Named_Funding_Covers_The_Maximum_Reachable_Buildout.md).

## Residual risk after mitigation

Still High for the first three years. The unlock programs have lead times of their own: transformer
plants and GOES steel capacity take years to build, which is the same problem one layer up.
