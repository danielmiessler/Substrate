# RI-00003 — The Plan Automates the Jobs It Trains People For

**Category:** Risk
**Created:** 2026-07-24
**Applies to:** [SO-00002](../Solutions/SO-00002—Domestic_Autonomy_Manufacturing_Push.md), [SO-00003](../Solutions/SO-00003—Universal_Free_AI_And_Business_Training.md)
**Severity:** High
**Likelihood:** Medium

---

## The risk

[SO-00002](../Solutions/SO-00002—Domestic_Autonomy_Manufacturing_Push.md) sets a target of
quadrupling US robot density from **307 to 1,220 per 10,000 manufacturing employees**
([IFR](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/robot-density-surges-in-europe-asia-and-americas)) —
roughly **1.15 million additional robots** across a workforce of **12.6 million**
([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)). At the same time,
[SO-00003](../Solutions/SO-00003—Universal_Free_AI_And_Business_Training.md) trains 50 million
adults, in part for jobs in that same sector.

Two pillars of one plan pointing in opposite directions is not automatically a contradiction, but
it is automatically a risk.

## The case that it is not a contradiction

Korea has the world's highest robot density at **1,220 per 10,000** and has not deindustrialized.
Density measures automation per worker, not workers displaced; high-density economies tend to have
larger manufacturing sectors, not smaller ones. The buildout also creates roughly
**3.1 million energy jobs** ([CL-00003](../Claims/CL-00003—The_Buildout_Employs_Over_Three_Million_People.md))
that are construction and grid work, which is among the hardest work to automate.

## The case that it is

The 3.1M figure is a **build-phase** workforce. When the ladder ends in 2036, the construction
demand ends with it, and the automation does not. A training program that spent a decade producing
electricians for a finite buildout leaves a cohort with a credential and no demand — the exact
failure mode of every previous federal retraining effort measured at
**$1,500–2,500 per participant with under 50% receiving training at all**
([TCF](https://tcf.org/content/report/beyond-job-placement-reimagining-wioa-for-economic-mobility-and-workforce-resilience/)).

## Mitigation built into the plan

[SO-00003](../Solutions/SO-00003—Universal_Free_AI_And_Business_Training.md) Tier 3 splits between
trades and **independent business formation**, and Tiers 1 and 2 are AI capability rather than
sector training. Capability transfers across sectors; a trade credential does not. This is the
reason the training pillar is not simply an electrician program, and the reason its cheapest tier
reaches the most people.

## Leading indicator

Ratio of T3 graduates entering sustained self-employment versus trades placement, tracked at 24
months. If trades placement dominates through 2032, the program is building a cohort with a 2036
expiry date.

## Residual risk

High after 2033, when the rate ladder plateaus and the composition of demand shifts from building
capacity to operating it.
